LAGOS RAIL MASS TRANSIT (LRMT) RED LINE

SECURITY & SURVEILLANCE REPORT - 2025

Prepared by: PUBLIC TRANSPORT – RAIL DEPARTMENT SECURITY & SURVEILLANCE UNIT

Date: August, 2025

Stations Covered: Agege, Ikeja, Oshodi, Mushin, Iju, Yaba, Oyingbo,

Objective and Activities

To routinely assess, monitor, and strengthen security measures across all Red Line stations by:

Activities:

  • 1. Monitoring the conduct, presence and activities of deployed security personnel, including LNSC, Police, and TTC security.
  • 2. Ensuring the clearance and safeguarding of LAMATA assets and infrastructure.
  • 3. Conducting quarterly evaluations, engagements, and training sessions for security personnel, usually on Saturdays to compliance with security protocols.
  • 4. Retrieving and Delivering surveillance and security documentations for incident reporting and record-keeping, in line with ISO standards.

Objectives:

  • Verifying deployment and conduct of security personnel.
  • Identifying vulnerabilities and safety threats to commuters, staff, and infrastructure.
  • Ensuring compliance with security standards and protocols.
  • Recommending corrective actions for continuous improvement and safer commuter experience.

Executive Summary

General Issues & Challenges:

  • Absence of Surveillance Systems and Security detection systems(e.g body scanners, meal detectors eal).
  • Inadequate Security Personnel (e.g., LNSC Marshals and Police Officers).
  • Misconducts by Some Security Personnel.
  • Lack of Dedicated Security Offices or Shelters, esp. during weekend and raining season.

Comprehensive actions were taken and recommendations were made for immediate improvements

General Security Recommendations for All Stations

    1. Increase Security Personnel Deployment
  • Strengthen security presence across all stations and critical points.
    • 2. Upgrade Perimeter and Access Control
  • Enhance fencing, gates, and controlled entry points to restrict unauthorized access.
    • 3. Install Surveillance and Monitoring Systems
  • Deploy CCTV cameras and monitoring tools for effective surveillance.
    • 4. Improve Lighting and Visibility
  • Ensure adequate illumination at stations and along corridors to deter crime and enhance safety.
    • 5. Provide Shelters for Security Personnel
  • Establish dedicated offices or shelters to support deployed security staff.
    • 6. Address Miscreant and Loitering Issues
  • Implement strict measures to curb loitering, illegal trading, and miscreant activities.
    • 7. Prevent Facility Encroachment and Misuse
  • Enforce proper utilization of facilities and prevent encroachment, especially in areas like Agege and Mushin TOD.

Guards equipped with necessary security tools to address challenges.

Oshodi Station

  • Significant shortfall in TTC guard deployment
  • Existing LNSC manpower insufficient to cover critical access points, particularly platform areas where unauthorized boarding attempts occur.
  • Risk of passengers climbing platforms from the tracks remains high due to limited surveillance and personnel coverage.

Current Status Update:

  • Under-deployment during night hours increases vulnerability of staff and station equipment.
  • Perimeter and platform access control is inadequate, enabling trespassing from trackside.

Unrestrained Pedestrian access across tracks as open gate allows free track access by Passersby

Recommendations:
• Increase LNSC deployment, particularly during peak hours and at night, with focus on platform security.
• Request police attachment for continuous law enforcement presence.
• Deploy additional night guards or shift existing manpower to bolster night coverage.
• Install surveillance cameras to monitor trackside access points and deter platform climbing.
• Improve perimeter fencing or install physical deterrents (spiked fencing, anti-climb panels) to reduce unauthorized entry from tracks.

General Security Recommendations for All Stations

    1. Increase Security Personnel Deployment
  • Deploy additional LNSC officers, particularly during high passenger flow periods and at night, to improve deterrence and rapid response.
  • Ensure adequate presence of police personnel for armed backup at strategic points.
    • 2. Upgrade Perimeter and Access Control
  • Repair and reinforce all damaged fences, gates, and barriers to prevent unauthorized entry.
  • Install higher fencing or anti-climb measures where low barriers are being exploited.
  • Replace faulty padlocks and improve locking mechanisms for all station gates.
    • 3. Enhance Surveillance and Monitoring Systems
  • Install CCTV cameras to ensure full coverage of platforms, entrances, pedestrian bridges, and vulnerable areas.
    • 4. Improve Lighting and Visibility
  • Install adequate lighting, especially on pedestrian bridges, station surroundings, and other high-risk areas to deter criminal activity.
  • Enforce walkway cleanliness and anti-hawking regulations with mobile patrols
    • 5. Provision of Shelter, Security Tools and Equipment
  • Equip guards with standard security tools such as batons, radios, flashlights, and protective gear for effective handling of incidents.
  • Provide dedicated security shelters at all stations
    • 6. Address Miscreant and Loitering Challenges
  • Maintain regular patrols to prevent illegal loitering and stop miscreants from gaining access to stations and surrounding areas.
  • Work with local law enforcement to remove persistent offenders from station environments.
    • 7. Facility Utilization and Encroachment Prevention
  • Put unused station property or land to productive use (e.g bus depots) to deter trespassing and illegal occupation.
