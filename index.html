LRMT Blue Line Surveillance and Monitoring Performance:

  • Surveillance Cameras: There are a total of 305 surveillance cameras across the 5 Blue line stations.​ With 5 control rooms and 1 command center at LAMATA PLACE.
  • 15 surveillance officers are deployed to the stations to ensure 100% monitoring of the surveillance system during operational hours.​
  • A total of 63 cameras were cleaned in the month of July by stations electricians and supervised by LAMATA surveillance officers.
  • The surveillance system, along with the eagle-eyed diligent officers, has enhanced monitoring, issue detection, and operations across all train stations

Key Achievement through Surveillance System

  • The apprehension of thieves at National Theatre.
  • Recovery of missing items and post-incidents intelligence to aid ivestigations through surveillance camera footage playback

Challenges:

  • Latency in surveillance camera feeds.​
  • Absence of communication system between station surveillance rooms and LAMATA control Centre.​
  • Absence of maintenance contract for surveillance system maintenance

Action Taken:

In the absence of a maintenance contract, our unit has been carrying out basic maintenance functions, including cleaning cameras and attending to minor technical issues.

Key Recommendations

Priority Actions:

  • Implement a Maintenance Schedule: Develop a structured plan for surveillance systems and body scanners across all Blue Line stations, ensuring regular inspections, servicing, and prompt repairs.
  • Enhance Crowd Control Deploy additional uniformed security personnel at high-traffic stations, such as Marina, during peak hours to effectively manage crowds and maintain order.
